## Sound & Scene History

Emerging out of late-night sessions around the **North Wollongong** industrial strip, **hxxvyweight** has been a key figure driving raw, high-BPM techno and hardgroove textures through the Illawarra underground.

### Key Milestones
* **Warehouse Roots:** Early pop-up sets across improvised spaces and industrial backlots.
* **Rad Basement Era:** Select residency sets focusing on late-90s European vinyl imports and relentless rhythm tracks.
* **Club Selects:** Frequent appearances across local hubs like *La La La's* on Globe Lane.

> "The aim has always been keeping it uncompromised—heavy low-end, fast tempos, and strictly zero commercial fluff."
