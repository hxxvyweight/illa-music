# The Grand Hotel

Nestled right on Crown Street amidst the hum of the city center, **The Grand Hotel** stands as a bona fide cornerstone of the Wollongong nightlife and live music circuit, carrying decades of local history within its walls while continuously serving as the sweat-soaked, unpretentious epicenter where generations of Illawarra punters, touring musicians, and late-night stragglers have collided over warm beer and walls of distorted sound.
