# Rock

Rooted in 1940s and 1950s rock and roll—itself drawing heavily from blues, rhythm and blues, and country music—**Rock** is defined by its driving backbeat, electric guitars, bass, and drums. Over the decades, it has branched into countless sub-genres like garage rock, punk, grunge, and psych-rock.

In the Illawarra, rock has long served as the working-class backbone of the local live pub circuit, anchoring historic venues and nurturing generations of garage bands.

## Core Characteristics

* **Instrumentation:** Electric guitars, bass guitar, drum kit, and vocals. Often augmented by keyboards or synthesizers.
* **The Groove:** Anchored by a strong, driving 4/4 rhythm with an emphasis on the backbeats (beats 2 and 4).
* **Attitude & Energy:** Ranging from polished arena anthems to raw, distorted, high-energy garage performances.
