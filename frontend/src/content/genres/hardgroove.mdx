# Hardgroove

Characterized by its relentless, infectious percussive loops, tribal and organic undertones, and a swinging 909-heavy groove that sits firmly in the 138 to 145 BPM range, **Hardgroove** has established itself as a vital sub-genre driving late-night warehouse sets and underground club nights across the Illawarra. 

Moving away from the sterile, industrial coldness of peak-time minimal techno, the style champions a warmer, more funk-infused low end designed explicitly to lock a dancefloor into a hypnotic, rolling momentum.

## Core Characteristics

* **Rolling Percussion:** Relies heavily on layered congas, shakers, bongos, and snappy hats that push the rhythm forward relentlessly.
* **Warm Sub-Bass:** Bypasses harsh, abrasive digital distortion for rounder, funkier basslines that breathe with the kick drum.
* **Loop-Driven Energy:** Built around tightly packed rhythmic loops that build tension through subtle filter sweeps and percussion dropouts rather than aggressive drops.
* **BPM:** Typically sits briskly between 132 and 145+ BPM, maintaining high physical energy.

## Scene Impact & Local Presence

While it took a backseat during various minimal and industrial waves, hardgroove has experienced a massive global and regional resurgence. Its high-energy, danceable framework makes it a favorite for modern underground club lineups in regional hubs looking to bridge the gap between classic techno drive and contemporary party energy.
